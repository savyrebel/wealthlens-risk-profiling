PROFILE RECONCILIATION
How we calculated this
1. Risk Capacity

Objective financial characteristics are used to estimate your ability to withstand losses.

2. Risk Tolerance

Your responses to the willingness and loss-reaction questions form the tolerance assessment.

3. Risk Perception

Product-level risk ratings are treated as a diagnostic, not as a direct reason to increase risk.

4. Risk Required

Your stated goal information is used to estimate the return required where enough numerical data is available.

5. Reconciliation

The final profile is constrained by financial capacity. A high willingness to take risk cannot by itself override inadequate financial capacity.
